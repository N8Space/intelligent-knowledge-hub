# Standard Operating Procedure: HIPAA Privacy Verification & Authorized Representative Disclosures
**Document ID:** `SOP-HIPAA-2026-001`  
**Classification:** Enterprise Compliance & Legal Mandate  
**Department:** Privacy Office & Customer Contact Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Regulatory Authority:** HIPAA Privacy Rule 45 CFR Part 160/164  

---

### Section 1: Member Caller Identity Verification Protocol
Before disclosing any Protected Health Information (PHI) or Protected Claims Data, the CSR must verify at least **3 of the following 4 primary identifiers**:
1. Full Legal Name of the Member.
2. Member Identification Number (or Social Security Number last 4 digits).
3. Date of Birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Full Residential Address on file (including Zip Code).

---

### Section 2: Authorized Third-Party & Personal Representative Verification
* **Spouse / Adult Dependent (>18 Years):** Under HIPAA, spouses and adult dependents cannot access each other's medical claims or clinical details without an active **HIPAA Authorization on File (Form PHI-AUTH-01)** or designated Medical Power of Attorney (POA).
* **Minor Dependents (<18 Years):** Parents or legal guardians may access routine claims except for state-mandated confidential services (reproductive health, substance treatment, mental health therapy where state age of consent applies).
* **Verbal 1-Time Authorization:** Allowed only if the authenticated primary member is on the active telephone line and explicitly grants verbal consent to disclose specific claim details during that single call session.
* **CSR Script for Unauthorized Caller:**
  > *"To protect member privacy in compliance with federal HIPAA privacy regulations, I am unable to discuss specific medical details or claims without an authorized release on file. I can help you submit our confidential Representative Authorization form today or speak with the member directly."*
