# Standard Operating Procedure: Prior Authorization & Clinical Appeals Workflow
**Document ID:** `SOP-UM-2026-004`  
**Classification:** Enterprise Clinical Operations / CSR Guideline  
**Department:** Utilization Management (UM) & Member Appeals  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Compliance Standard:** NCQA / URAC / CMS Timeliness Regulations  

---

### Section 1: Prior Authorization (PA) Review Timelines & SLAs
1. **Standard Non-Urgent Pre-Service Request:**
   * Decision determination communicated within **72 clock hours** (3 business days) of receiving complete clinical documentation.
   * If clinical notes are incomplete, a formal Request for Information (RFI) must be issued within 24 hours giving provider 45 days to respond.
2. **Expedited / Urgent Review (Imminent Health Threat):**
   * Review determination required within **24 clock hours** of receipt when the treating physician certifies standard turnaround could jeopardize patient health or pain management.
3. **Concurrent Inpatient Review:**
   * Decision communicated within **24 hours** of request.

---

### Section 2: Clinical Denial Escalation & Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Protocol
* **Peer-to-Peer Window:** The ordering clinician may request a Peer-to-Peer (P2P) discussion with a Plan Medical Director within **5 business days** of an adverse determination notice.
* **P2P Scheduling:** CSR can initiate P2P scheduling via UM queue `UM-CLIN-P2P`. The medical director must hold the call within 2 business days of request.
* **Level 1 Member Appeal SLA:**
   * Standard Appeal: Decision within **30 calendar days** for pre-service; **60 calendar days** for post-service claims.
   * Expedited Member Appeal: Decision within **72 clock hours**.
* **CSR Script for Denied Prior Auth Inquiry:**
  > *"I understand your physician recommended this treatment. At this time, additional clinical records were needed by our medical review board. Your doctor has the option to request a Peer-to-Peer discussion with our Medical Director within 5 business days, or our team can assist you in filing an expedited Level 1 appeal right now."*
