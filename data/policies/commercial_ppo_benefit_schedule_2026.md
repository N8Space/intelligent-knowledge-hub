# Plan Document: Commercial Premier & Standard PPO Benefit Schedule (Plan Year 2026)
**Document ID:** `PLAN-PPO-2026-BENEFITS`  
**Classification:** Enterprise Internal / Health Plan Operations  
**Line of Business:** Commercial Group & Individual PPO  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Regulatory Authority:** ACA / ERISA / State Insurance Commissioner  

---

### Section 1: Annual Deductibles and Out-of-Pocket Maximums (OOPM)
* **Standard PPO In-Network Individual Deductible:** $1,500 per calendar year ($3,000 Family).
* **Standard PPO Out-of-Network Individual Deductible:** $3,000 per calendar year ($6,000 Family).
* **In-Network Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM):** $6,000 Individual / $12,000 Family (includes deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance).
* **Out-of-Network OOPM:** $12,000 Individual / $24,000 Family. Balance billing amounts from non-participating providers do not accumulate toward OOPM.

---

### Section 2: Preventive Care & Diagnostic Imaging Cost Shares
* **ACA Mandated Preventive Services:** Covered at 100% (No member cost share / $0 Copay) when rendered by an In-Network participating provider.
* **Routine Primary Care Visit:** $25 Copay (deductible waived).
* **Specialist Physician Consultation:** $50 Copay (deductible waived; no Primary Care Physician referral required for PPO).
* **Advanced Diagnostic Imaging (MRI, CT, PET Scan):**
  * **In-Network:** Subject to deductible, then 20% coinsurance at participating freestanding imaging centers (30% at hospital outpatient facility).
  * **Out-of-Network:** Subject to out-of-network deductible, then 40% coinsurance plus balance billing. Requires prior authorization except for emergency evaluations.

---

### Section 3: Prescription Drug Formulary Tiers
* **Tier 1 (Preferred Generic):** $10 Copay (30-day retail supply) / $20 Copay (90-day mail order).
* **Tier 2 (Preferred Brand):** $40 Copay after pharmacy deductible.
* **Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand):** $85 Copay after pharmacy deductible.
* **Tier 4 (Specialty Biologics):** 20% Coinsurance up to maximum $250 per prescription fill; mandatory fulfillment through Plan Specialty Pharmacy.
